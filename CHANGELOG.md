# Changelog

## Unreleased: Symposium 3

- **Timeline: the list of timelines**: a list of the open artboard's timelines now sits beside the Timeline. It shows each one's length, loop mode, which layers it animates and what starts it ("Nothing plays it" when nothing does), marks the ones that animate the selected layer, and lets you rename, reorder, duplicate and delete them there (deleting warns when something plays the timeline). On a component it offers the Idle, Hover and Pressed timelines that are missing. The List button on the Timeline bar shows or hides it.
- **Timeline: Sound and captions fold**: Sound, Captions and Descriptions now fold into one "Sound and captions" row, closed on a timeline with no sound or captions and showing a mark for each clip and caption. The new View menu hides any of the three lanes. Both choices are remembered for you.
- **Data files open in a Form**: a JSON data file now opens in a Form instead of a tree. Key names become plain labels (`startLabel` reads "Start label"), each group is a card of fields, and a list of groups such as dilemmas or choices is a stack of cards you can open, close, add another to (new cards copy the fields of the ones already there, left empty), copy, move up and down, and delete. An outline beside the form jumps to any card. Adding a new field, renaming one or changing what a field holds stays in Tree, and every card has an "In Tree" button that opens it right there. Tree, Table and Raw text are still one click away, and CSV and TSV still open as a table.
- **Voice for the word: Play**: a **Play** button under Word audio plays the word's current audio (made or your own) without leaving the Inspector. It reads Stop while it plays, only one sound plays at a time, and it stops when you move to another word or page or the audio changes.
- **Voice for the word: keep or discard a recording**: **Record it now** no longer saves the moment you press Stop. You can **Listen** to the take, then **Use this recording** (one undo step) or **Discard** it, and nothing is uploaded or added to Assets until you use it. Moving to another word discards an unused take; closing the editor with one asks first.
- **Code editor colours**: Scripts, the Data tab's raw text and the other code boxes can now follow the editor's light or dark theme (the new default), or be set to Light or Dark on their own. Pick it with the Code colours buttons above the code or in Preferences; it is your own setting, not saved in the project.
- **Edit data files in the editor**: a data source that is an uploaded file can now be changed right in the Data tab, as plain text (coloured for JSON, and a colour for each column of a CSV or TSV, with a mistake marked at its line and column), as a table (CSV, TSV, or a JSON list of rows) or as a tree you can open and close (other JSON, with lists of rows opening as tables). Add, copy, delete and move rows, columns and items, and change a value between text, number, yes/no, empty, list and group. Changes save a moment after you stop typing as a new version of the file, so Undo, other people editing and the canvas all follow; JSON that doesn't parse is not saved and the last good version keeps running. A file that several data sources share says so before you edit it and can be given its own copy, and a data source from a URL can be viewed and turned into an uploaded copy to edit.
- **Page changes**: twelve more looks on Go to a page: star, diamond, heart and clock (opening from the clicked layer), barn doors, a diagonal wipe, blinds, a grid of squares, crosshatch, dots, dissolve and a turning cube. All of them take the speed presets and are instant with reduced motion.
- **Question cards**: a question's own button (Play the word, Repeat, a clip's Play) now leads the card in the accent colour, with Submit answer at the far right, so the two never look alike in any theme.
- **Page change speed**: wherever you choose how a page changes (Go to a page, When clicked, Page navigation and a layer's Go to page), a **Speed** of Slow, Medium, Fast or Custom (the milliseconds) sits beside the look, and is left out for Instant. Medium is the 300 ms every page change already had, so nothing in an existing project plays differently; what is stored is still the plain number. The Inspector's sentences say it too, as in "go to the next page (iris, slow)".
- **More page transitions**: pages can now wipe in four directions, push, open an iris from the clicked layer, or zoom into it, chosen wherever you pick a page change (Go to a page, When clicked, Page navigation and a layer's link). Reduced motion still makes every change instant.
- **Soundtrack music**: tick **Soundtrack** on a Play sound and the music carries on across pages, goes quiet under narration and Speak, crossfades to a different soundtrack, and fades out at Stop sound or start over. **When clicked…** has *Play music that carries on across pages* and *Stop the music*.
- **Depth (parallax)**: give a layer a **Depth** (None, Small, Medium, Large, or any percent) and it shifts a little as the viewer moves the pointer, nearer layers more than far ones, for a layered-postcard look. It shows in Preview and the exported player, never on the canvas while editing, and not at all for viewers who ask for less motion. **Project settings** has the **Depth range** and an off-by-default **Tilt moves depth layers** option for phones (iPhones ask permission on the first tap, never on load; a refusal falls back to the pointer).
- **Move the view to a layer**: pan and zoom a page to frame one of its layers, with space around it, a duration and easing, then go back to the whole page. For a map the learner travels across, or a comic read panel by panel. The page master stays put, clicks and drags still land on the right layer, the player resizing keeps the layer in frame, changing page starts on the whole view, and a learner who asked for less motion jumps to the end. It is under **+ When clicked…** (**Move the view to a layer…**, **Show the whole page again**), in the Interactions palette under Navigation, and as `ix.moveView` and `ix.resetView` in scripts.
- **Fill in this starter**: every branded starter has a **Fill in** panel beside its Checklist: plain fields and lists for its words, questions, answers, pictures and settings, so you change the content without going through Variables, layer names or nodes. Edits undo and reach collaborators like any other, and each checklist item has a **Fill in** button that opens the right field. The command palette can open it too.
- **Sharing made simple**: **Share…** asks **Where will people use it?** (a StudyDesk page keeping results, a StudyDesk page with no results, the gradebook, anyone with a link, or a file) and shows the one thing to copy, with every other option still under **More ways to share**. **Before you share** lists what's likely wrong first (buttons that do nothing, pages nothing leads to, sample words left in, missing picture descriptions, contrast, broken links, files nothing uses), one line per kind of problem that opens to show each one with a button to fix or find it, and never stops you.
- **Paste questions from Word or Excel**: paste or open a file and see what was understood before adding it (multiple choice, multiple answer, true/false, fill in the blank, matching), with downloadable Word, Excel and CSV templates that have a worked example of each. **Ctrl/Cmd+K** is now a **command palette** for doing things (add, go to a page or layer, animate, behaviours, view, tools, panels, share and export), with help results too; F1 still searches the help. **Animation presets** on any layer: Fade in, Slide in, Pop, Pulse, Shake and Fade out, on page open or on click, written as ordinary timelines that people who prefer less motion see at their end pose.
- **Plain words**: the editor talks like an author: pages instead of artboards, and nodes such as **When clicked or hovered**, **Go to a page**, **Change a value or counter** and **Show or hide a layer** (the technical names are in the tooltips and search, and **Technical wording** in Preferences brings them back). Opacity and scale are **percents**, and the pivot is a 3×3 picker. Big changes (tidy, delete a page, imports, colourways…) leave a message with an **Undo** button. A new picture asks **What is this a picture of?** (Save, Decorative or Skip). **Crop** a picture inside its frame (double-click it). Variables come as Counter, Switch, Score, Text or List and say where they're used. An **Icons** tab inserts the Toolbox's own icons.
- **Starters, tidier still**: Back and Next sit in a bar along the bottom of every starter page (Back left, Next right). Checklist items are short, one job each, with **Why and how** folded underneath. The **Mind map** has a **Keep the learner's map** setting, with Save, Start a new map, Open a saved map and downloads (text, picture, or a copy to open later). The layout and contrast checks now also check content that fades in.
- **Interactions without the graph**: **+ When clicked…** (and When hovered) on a layer or the canvas menu offers plain behaviours: go to the next, previous or a chosen page, show, hide or toggle a layer, play an animation or a sound, count, switch on or off, open a link, start over. They're written as ordinary nodes you can still edit. A layer's interactions read as **sentences** ("When clicked: show Hint text, then wait 2 s"), with Show in graph, Turn off and Remove, and the graph has a **Read as sentences** view. Conditions (Only if, If, Only while) are built with **pickers** (variable, is / is at least / contains…, value) with **Edit as formula** one click away, and every formula field says what's wrong in plain words with a one-click fix ("Did you mean score?") or shows its current value.
- **A gentler start**: someone new is asked **What do you want to make?** and offered a starter to try first; a **Getting started** strip ticks off the first five jobs; the starter picker has search, goals instead of jargon, and **Play** on every starter; and a new **Essentials** workspace shows only what a first project needs (the rest is one click away under More panels and More tools).
- **See what ran**: Preview can sit **beside the editor**, and while it plays the nodes that ran light up in the graph, the wire they followed pulses, and the layer you clicked rings green (or red, with **Why did nothing happen?** in the events list). **Link a page by dragging**: a selected layer has a Go to page handle you drag onto a page (or pick from a list), and faint arrows show how pages link. **Page navigation in one click**: Back, Next and a counter, dots or progress bar along the bottom of one page or all of them. The Interactions panel opens taller when you start building, and goes back after.
- **Shared responses**: a data source that gathers learners' short answers, set to **Only me** or **Everyone in this class**, with counts (never names), blocked words, a minimum before an answer shows, per-person limits and the owner able to hide, remove or clear answers. The **Word cloud** starter's class cloud runs on it (a page shared before this needs sharing again).
- **Course collections**: named stamps and counters (a passport, a badge case) that every interactive in the same StudyDesk course shares for each learner. **Add to**, **Load** and **Check course collection** nodes and `ix.course`; a stamp is kept once and a counter keeps the larger number, so two pieces can't take each other back. On the StudyDesk learner page they are kept on the server by the signed-in learner and course; in Preview, an export or outside StudyDesk there is no course, so they are kept in that browser only, and Preview's toolbar says so.
- **Every starter's graphs are annotated**, and the examples' bigger graphs too. Notes draw `code` inside **bold** properly, and a [ ] partway through a line shows as a box.
- **Graph notes in the starters**: every quiz, drag and explore starter's interaction graphs now carry short notes saying what each chain does, why it's built that way and what to change. **Word cloud** settings: **Pills** on or off and **Word colour** plain or following the colourway. **Folded Properties sections** follow you to any computer you sign in on.
- **F1 searches the help again** (as does Ctrl+K); **?** and the Help button open the page for what you're working on. The **Media Catalogue** button shows the catalogue's iris icon.
- **Connectors**: a **Connector** tool (K) draws a line or arrow from one layer to another that follows them when they move: straight, elbow (it routes around both boxes) or curved, with arrowheads or dots and an optional label. Connectors can join copies of a repeated layer from a list of links, and redraw in the player, Preview and every export.
- **Mind map and Flowchart starters**: a **Mind map** to explore (ideas fan out from the middle, fold, open for detail, move with the arrow keys, read as an outline) and one learners build and download; a **Flowchart** to follow (answer each decision and the route lights up, with a numbered text version) and a **complete the flowchart** activity. Both are laid out from lists, so you edit words, not shapes.
- **Small buttons** keep their size: a button narrower than about 80 px used to grow by its padding, so a dot-sized button showed a white sliver beside it (the Dated timeline's event dots).
- **Keyboard focus you can always see**: the focus ring is two-tone (the colourway's focus colour with a thin black or white edge), so it shows on white pages, dark gradients and deep panels alike, and buttons, inputs and links now show it too instead of the browser's own ring. The starter contrast check now holds focus rings on every page strictly.
- **Interactions panel**: an **artboard picker** at the top (with each page's node count) that follows the canvas both ways; **search** the nodes by name, what they do or a typo ("delay" finds Wait), with Events / Actions / Logic chips and 15 foldable topics that remember how you left them. Every node's description is now **one sentence** with a **More about… →** link to its full help. **Notes in the graph**: sticky notes beside nodes and groups that follow them, like canvas notes, and never shipped to players (three starters carry sample graph notes so far).
- **Starters, refined**: the **Spelling test** turns Play off while the word plays, says **Submit answer**, and has switches for **Try again** and **Hint** (a small pill at the top right). Every question starter now has the hint pill and the same switches, buttons say Submit where the answer counts and Check where it's practice, and the Lite lesson's transcript is a pill. **Contrast**: every starter page, in every state and colourway, now meets WCAG AA for text, button edges, progress bars and chart marks (muted text, borders and the feedback colours are a little darker, Purple gets a purple highlight, the focus ring follows the colourway, and Orange and Red title pages have a light shade behind white words); a strict test keeps it that way. The Dated timeline's labels sit on their own chips.
- **Help that knows where you are**: the Help button (and F1) opens the help for what you're working on: the field you're in, the selected node, tool, layer type, artboard or panel, and its tooltip says which. The help drawer has **Back, Forward and Home**, and remembers where you were on each page; Ctrl+K searches it. The extra **?** at the bottom of the tool bar is gone (right-click the tool bar for its help). **Preview** sits above everything, traps the keyboard while it's open and opens help in a new window. The **Properties** panel remembers which sections you folded, with fold and unfold all. **Home** from a project always goes back to your Projects, with the one you left highlighted. The **Media Catalogue** button uses the catalogue's own icon and lives in the **Media** tools group.
- **New starters**: a **Word cloud** (an author's cloud of weighted words with details, and a learner's own cloud with a blocklist; a "Who sees it" setting is ready for a class-wide cloud) and a **Dated timeline** (events on a real date scale with eras, zoom from decades to months, labels that never collide, a portrait layout, a list view, and a put-in-order activity). Both have keyboard and list alternatives and pass the layout and contrast checks.
- **Snapping menu**: the Snap button opens a menu to choose what things snap to: **grid, artboard, layers, guides, spacing** (equal gaps, with distance labels), **whole pixels** and **path points**, with a master switch and the snap distance; the same choices are in Preferences and the canvas menu, and the guide line says what it snapped to. Resizing and drawing now snap to artboards, layers and guides too. **Rulers** make the guide you'd expect: the top ruler a horizontal one, the left ruler a vertical one. Panels and the tool bar can dock in a new **top zone** under the header. An input's value is now an ordinary **⇄ Both** binding (older projects are converted when opened).
- **Download a file works in any project**, not only ones that use the microphone or camera, so the journal and workbook now offer **Download as a file** instead of an email link (shares made before this need **Update to the latest version**). **Keyboard navigation** follows its index variable when something else moves it, can keep hidden items' numbers (the crossword is now a keyboard grid that steps over blank squares), and Focus on a list lands on its current item. Arrow-key **shortcuts** work however the browser reports the key. The **Accordion** starter is one repeated section, and the chart starter's guess bar is a dashed "?" whose value can't leak.
- **Canvas fixes**: a results page that reads `quiz` (score, max, the questions) shows numbers on the canvas instead of its formula in huge type; notes are drawn at the canvas's own zoom, so zoomed out they no longer spread over neighbouring notes and artboards; **Tidy up artboards** leaves room for each artboard's notes; a page master's locked brand sweeps no longer show their faint overhang across the canvas.
- **Media and practice starters rebuilt**: one **record or stop** button and **Play/Stop** buttons that know when a sound is playing, so "model then mine" never plays your take after you stop the model; empty videos say so themselves, the audio-only lesson is a video layer with a poster, and Observe and tag uses the video's own captions and transcript. Timed talk uses the **Countdown / clock**, Keep the beat one steady timer, the sound lab a native **meter**, and Sign practice one video that changes with the sign. **Every starter now passes the layout check in every colourway**, and the check fails the build if a button lands on text or anything crowds the logo again.
- **Fixes from the sweep**: repeated layers that hug their height shrink when a child is hidden; a chart can **hide a value** (a dashed "?" bar for a guess) and take `{variables}` in its prefix and suffix; the selected row in **Layers** is gold like every other chosen state, and the rows can be reached and moved through with the keyboard.
- **Games, story and writing starters rebuilt**: the Arithmetic quiz and Word search use the **Countdown / clock**, Word search is a keyboard grid that knows which square you drag across, and the crossword's moves are worked out live. The game map, branching scenario and book open pages by name or expression and count pages themselves, with chapters as artboard groups. The course presentation steps with next and previous and the arrow keys. **Video questions** use video cues and chapters instead of watching the clock. The journal, workbook and questionnaire **save as they go** (no Save buttons) and pick up where the learner left off. Every page passes the layout check, with notes beside each starter.
- **Assessment starters rebuilt**: True or false, Single-choice set, Summary and Audio-first go through a **question bank once** and finish on a score, with **Practise the missed ones**. The multi-page quiz and spelling test total their questions with **Score from: all questions**, keep answers when a page is revisited and list results with a repeater; the spelling test switches the **spell checker guard** on. Fill the blanks is a real blanks question, Image choice and Mark the words use live counts and one repeated card or chip, and hotspot questions now use **one click flow for all their areas** (the question kit builds them that way too). Audio-first takes **shortcut keys** and announces when there is no voice. Every page passes the layout check, with notes beside each starter.
- **Drag and explore starters rebuilt**: Sort into groups is a **categorise** question, Label the diagram and Image pairing are **matching** questions with a distractor, and Drag the words is **fill the blanks by dragging**, so marking, tries and results come from the question (graphs went from up to 41 nodes to 7). The timeline, slider, hotspots, cards, wall and collage use one repeated layer and one click flow instead of a copy per item, with pressed and current states for screen readers; the hotspots open a proper dialog; the chart starter uses native chart layers. Every page passes the layout check, and each starter has notes beside its pages.
- **Examples, tidied**: every example's graphs and artboards are laid out neatly and carry a note or two saying what to look at. The hotspot question uses one click flow for all its areas, the Dusk poster's twenty stars are one repeated star, the joysticks link their pages with next and previous, the lamp's switch says when it is on, and text that ran into buttons or off the page has been moved. **Tidy up** in the interaction graph no longer moves a graph with a group in it further down each time.
- **Starters, part 1**: branded starters keep the UniSQ chrome on page masters (a quarter smaller overall, up to half for long ones), the logo has its proper clear space, page titles shrink to fit instead of running into the accent bar, and every starter's graphs and artboards come out tidy. **Essay** is rebuilt on the new features: the word count, length check and keyword feedback are live (the feedback page follows the answer without submitting again), a progress bar shows the length, the pages lay themselves out so long prompts and model answers never reach the buttons, and notes beside the pages explain how it works.
- **Spelling tests**: each word can use **My own audio** (pick, upload or record it) instead of a made voice, and switching keeps both. A new **Spell checkers and writing tools** setting switches off the browser's spell check and autocorrect, blocks paste, and watches for Grammarly-style extensions: **Block** holds the learner on a screen that says how to turn the tool off until it's gone, **Warn only** just tells them. A teacher code lets a learner who needs their tools through, and what was found is kept with their result. Phone keyboards and second devices can't be seen, and the help says so.
- **Properties search**: a search box at the top of the Properties panel finds a setting by name, by what it does or with a typo ("transparency" finds Opacity), showing only the matching rows. Count pills are readable everywhere (dark text, a pale pill on the gold row). The **?** in panel tab rows has moved to the tab's right-click menu (**Help for this panel**), and the Help button opens the help for the panel you're in. Panels no longer clip their selects and notes at the default sizes.
- **Notes**: the **Note** tool (S) leaves sticky notes on the canvas for the people making a project, on an artboard, beside it or following a layer, with light formatting and tick boxes. They come with copies and templates and reach collaborators live; players, Preview, share links and pictures never show them. An eye button shows or hides them.
- **Page masters**: one artboard can hold what every page shares (logo, accent bar, footer) and pages draw it underneath their own layers, so a change to the brand is one edit and projects are much smaller. Pages can leave master layers off and fill `{slots}` in its text.
- **Progress bars and charts**: a **progress** layer (bar or meter, read out by screen readers) and a **chart** layer (column, bar, line, pie, donut) drawn from a list, with theme colours, hover and keyboard readouts and a data table for screen readers. Text in repeated layers now takes its real size, so lists of varying text lay out properly.
- **Grid**: **Show grid** (Shift+G) and **Snap to grid** (Ctrl+') on the tool rail, with grid sizes, stronger lines, a per-artboard grid, and the same options in the canvas menu and Preferences. The **Tools** rail can be docked left, right or at the bottom by dragging it.
- **Media Catalogue**: a button that opens the photo catalogue and puts the photo you choose on the artboard. Shares and embeds link to it; single-file and package exports carry the picture itself.
- **Questions and dragging**: matching questions can have **extra terms** that belong nowhere; a new **Categorise** question (groups and cards, with feedback per drop); **Fill the blanks** can be answered **by dragging** words from a bank. A question bank can **go through each row once and finish**, with set totals and **review the missed ones**. **Score from: total of all questions** and the `quiz` names (score, percent, passed, a list of every question) make a results page without wiring; questions can **keep their answer** when the page is visited again and **hide Try again**. **Enable dragging** and **Place in zone** work on everything, a container's contents or a tag at once.
- **Pages and events**: **Go to artboard** can go to the **next**, **previous** or **back** page, or one worked out by an expression (with a **No artboard** output), and `pageNumber` / `pageCount` count the pages (per artboard group). Click, key and focus events say **which child** was hit (`event.child`, `event.childIndex`), every event has an **Only if** condition, drag events give the copy under the pointer, and **Focus** waits for a layer that has only just appeared. The **Timer** takes an expression, **Only while** and **steady** timing, and a new **Countdown / clock** node starts, pauses, resumes and resets a clock with a **Time up** output.
- **Accessibility**: disabled buttons and inputs look dimmed (per layer **Disabled look**); **Pressed** and **Current** states for toggle and tab buttons; frames can be **dialogs** (modal, scrim, Escape, focus kept inside and given back); **Keyboard navigation** on a list, grid or radio group (one Tab stop, arrows, Home and End); a **Shortcut** key on any layer.
- **Live variables**: tick **Keep up to date** on a formula variable and it is worked out again whenever something it reads changes (read only, circles flagged). **Save as user data** on a variable loads it before the project starts and saves it as it changes, with no Save/Load nodes. New formula helpers `wordCount`, `band`, `clock`, `shuffle`, `randInt`, `includesAll`/`includesAny` and a **Seconds to m:ss** converter. Set variable can **toggle an item**, **remove a value**, **set one item** (or one field of it) and **reset**; **Reset variables** puts several back in one node. **Wait** takes an expression.
- **Video and sound**: **cues** (with pause) and **chapters** on video layers and an **On video cue** event, **Hold paused**, a **poster** image (also for sound-only), and a "Choose a video" panel for an empty video. **Play sound** and **Play recording** have a **Stopped** output, and `soundPlaying()` says what is playing. **Start recording** can **toggle** (record or stop, quieting other sounds first). **Speak** can announce instead when there is no voice, and has **Only speak when**. Replacing a sound file updates its length and offers to fit the timeline.
- **Sign practice** offers the right three choices for every sign (they stayed on the first sign's).
- **Themes that change nothing say why**: switching theme on a project whose colours are all fixed shows "Nothing here follows the theme" with **Link colours to tokens**, which links every colour that matches a token (by role when two share a value) in one undo step and lists the rest. **+ Colourway** in the Themes tab adds any UniSQ colourway as a theme. A themed **loading spinner** while a project waits to show, and Preview opens without flashing.
- **Fonts served from our own server**: Google fonts are fetched once by the editor server and kept under `storage/_fonts` (Open Sans ready at start), so the editor, Preview, share pages and the StudyDesk learner page never contact Google; `INTERACTIVES_FONTS_PROXY=off` turns it off. **No font pop-in**: the player waits up to 1.5 s for the first page's fonts before showing, the canvas waits for an artboard's fonts, and artboard pictures are never made with a fallback font. **Fit artboard to selection** in the canvas right-click menu.
- **Several artboards at once**: Ctrl/Cmd+click or Shift+click artboard names (canvas) or rows (Layers) to choose several; move, align, distribute, tidy, duplicate or delete them together; **Group artboards** into named, foldable groups (a dashed frame with a gold name on the canvas), with context menu items on the canvas and in Layers. The canvas right-click menu's items now respond to clicks (they didn't).
- **Ctrl/Cmd+click in the interaction graph** adds a node to the selection or takes it out (it never reached the graph before).
- **Embed code** in Share link: an iframe in the same shape as the toolbox's own items (title, `data-cdt-id`, `allowfullscreen`, full width at the start artboard's aspect, `microphone; camera` when the project uses them), one code for StudyDesk, StaffDesk and the open site; the learner page passes the project's height up so the iframe resizes like other ILARs.
- **Preview keeps Save user data and remembered progress** between runs (Forget saved data starts again); **Ctrl+S** saves from anywhere in the editor; videos show their frame in artboard stills; the text toolbar keeps keyboard focus; a dragged layer bound **In** says to use **⇄ Both** to save where it was left.
- **Polish**: a theme-linked colour shows as a compact one-line chip (each theme's value in its tooltip); label columns line up in the Layer, corner radius, question and project sections; long labels stack over their box; browser-drawn selects and text boxes match the rest; text on gold rows is the palette's dark grey. The left panel starts wider (300 px, was 240), so its tabs, the help button and the Comments filters aren't clipped; a width you dragged to is kept.
- **Spelling test voice**: with Spelling on, a question's **Voice for the word** makes the word's audio with a synthesised voice on the server while you edit (eight British voices, speed, preview, "Say it like" respelling, an optional sentence, one word or all of them), saved as ordinary audio so the shared page still has no word in it. The voice model (132 MB, Apache-2.0) downloads once on the first **Set up the voice**. A labelled **practice mode** has the learner's own device say the word instead, and turns off server marking because the word is then in the page.
- Canvas: **Ctrl/Cmd+click** adds a layer to the selection (or takes it out), like Shift.
- **Fit text to box** on text layers, buttons and links: the font is the largest size between Smallest and Largest (either can be 'any') at which the text fits the box, re-fitted when the text, box, font or bound values change, the same on the canvas and the played page.
- **Expression suggestions as you type** in every expression field (bindings, graph nodes, variable formulas, `{...}` in text): variables with their current values, `context` and its fields, data sources, layer names inside `layer("`, `self.` and other members, built-in names and helper functions with their signatures. Arrows, Enter or Tab, Escape.
- **Starters open laid out** in order in a tidy grid (responsive variants beside their page), and **Tidy up artboards** (Layers panel, canvas right-click) does the same for any project in one undo step.
- **Brand-locked layers are really locked**: not selectable or draggable on the canvas or in the Layers panel (F9 still lifts it), and projects made before this are locked when they open.
- Chosen states, sliders, ticks and radios use the palette gold everywhere; the Use template dialog keeps its name and buttons in view and scrolls the rest.
- Video layers: playback speed (Speed setting, a `media.rate` binding, learner Slower/Faster buttons that keep the pitch) and frame step (buttons and the , . keys), and a Control video node (play, pause, seek, skip, step a frame, set speed).
- **Play sound** takes volume, speed (pitch kept), loop and 'stop other sounds first', and its sound can come from an expression; a new **Stop sound** node ends loops. Sounds follow the viewer's mute.
