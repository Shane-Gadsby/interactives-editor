# Paths and paints

Paths: paths: [{closed, points: [{x, y, in?: {x,y}, out?: {x,y}}]}] in the layer's own box. Paint: {type:"solid", color} | {type:"linear", angle, stops:[{offset, color}], from?: {x,y}, to?: {x,y}} | {type:"radial", stops, center?: {x,y}, radius?} | {type:"image", assetId, fit}. With from/to (or center/radius) a gradient sits exactly there, in px from the layer's top-left, instead of running corner to corner along the angle; they can be animated as `fill.from.x`, `fill.to.y`, `fill.center.x`, `fill.radius` (also `stroke.paint.…`, `fills.0.…`). Stroke: {paint, width, dash?, trimStart?, trimEnd?, trimOffset?, cap?, join?, align?: "center"|"inside"|"outside"}. Several fills and strokes: fill/stroke is the first (bottom) one and fills: Paint[] / strokes: Stroke[] hold the ones painted on top, bottom to top. All fills are under all strokes unless paintOrder says otherwise: paintOrder: ("fill"|"stroke")[], one entry per paint from the bottom up, e.g. ["stroke","fill"] draws the stroke under the fill (left out = plain order). Any paint or stroke may also have opacity (0-1), blend, visible (false hides it), feather (soft blur, px) and offsetX/offsetY, so a drop shadow is an extra fill that is dark, see-through, feathered and offset. Paths for set_props and tracks: "fills.0.color", "fills.0.opacity", "strokes.0.width", "strokes.0.paint.color".
