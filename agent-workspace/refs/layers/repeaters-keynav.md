# Repeaters, repeat.select and keyboard navigation (keyNav)

A repeater (frame or group with repeat {items, layout, gap, columns}) can carry repeat.select {mode: one|several, variable (variable id: a number from 0, -1 for none, when one; a list of numbers when several), toggle (one only: clicking the picked copy clears it)}: a click, Space or Enter on a copy picks it with no nodes, each copy sees isSelected (and item.selected on record items), copies become radios or checkboxes with aria-checked, arrows move between them. A frame or group can carry keyNav {mode: list|grid|radio, orientation: vertical|horizontal|both, columns (grid on a container; a grid repeater uses its own), wrap, indexVariable (variable id, number from 0; setting it moves focus too when it is inside), countHidden (hidden copies keep their numbers and are stepped over)}: one item is the Tab stop and the arrows, Home and End move between a repeater's copies or a container's children; radio mode also selects (clicks) the item it lands on.
