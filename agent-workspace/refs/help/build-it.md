# Build it: tutorials by kind of activity
Step-by-step tutorials for the things people make most: questions, cards, sliders, stories, games, journals and more, each with its finished project to open.

Each tutorial builds one kind of activity from a blank project, one step at a time. Do [Tutorial 1](tutorial-first-animation) and [Tutorial 2](tutorial-first-interaction) first if you haven't used the editor before.

**Prefer cards?** The **Tutorials** button on the projects page lists all of these with their pictures, how long each takes, and your progress and badges. See [Tutorials, badges and progress](help#tutorials-badges-and-progress).

**Just want to look?** Every tutorial's finished project is on the projects page: open **Templates**, and under **Examples** find **Build it**. **Play** runs it, and **Make my copy** gives you your own copy to pull apart.

## Beginner

- [A multiple choice question with scoring and feedback](tutorial-mcq), about 10 minutes. One question with four options, two tries, a hint, feedback for each wrong answer, and a result sent when it finishes.
- [Drag and drop matching](tutorial-drag-match), about 10 minutes. Drag each country onto its capital, with a country that belongs nowhere, three tries, part marks and Try again.
- [Click to reveal: image hotspots](tutorial-reveal), about 20 minutes. A picture with numbered points. Each opens a dialog with a heading, text and a picture, closed by a button or Esc, and a point turns green once it has been opened.
- [Flip cards from a list](tutorial-flipcards), about 15 minutes. Term and meaning cards that turn over on a click or Enter, all drawn by one card layer from one list, so adding a card is adding a line.
- [An image slider (carousel)](tutorial-carousel), about 20 minutes. Slides with Previous, Next, a dot for each slide and the arrow keys, plus an autoplay that pauses and never runs for people who ask for less motion.
- [Before and after compare](tutorial-compare), about 15 minutes. Two pictures stacked, with a divider handle you drag, or move with the keyboard, to show more of one or the other. No nodes: a handle, a variable and two bindings.
- [A course presentation](tutorial-presentation), about 15 minutes. Four slides that share a page master, a check your understanding question, and Back, Next and the page number added once, on the master, with a slide transition.
- [Your first visual novel](tutorial-visual-novel), about 15 minutes. Start from the Visual novel template, add a character with faces, write a scene with a choice, play it from a line and share it.
- [Add a puzzle](tutorial-add-puzzle), about 10 minutes. Put a puzzle in a story: a question in the puzzle frame, hints bought with hint coins, called from the script, with the story answering how it went.
- [Sound effects and music across pages](tutorial-sound), about 15 minutes. Music that starts on the first page and keeps playing on the next, a sound effect on a button, a sound on or off button and a button that stops the music.
- [Use the UniSQ look and colour themes](tutorial-brand), about 15 minutes. Give a page the UniSQ Purple colourway with theme tokens, see it in Light, Dark and High contrast, and know when to start from a branded starter pack instead.

## Intermediate

- [Fill in the blanks with typed answers](tutorial-blanks), about 15 minutes. Four gaps the learner types into, accepting other wordings, near numbers and a pattern, with part marks and a tip for one gap.
- [Sort into groups](tutorial-sort), about 12 minutes. Six cards to drag into two labelled groups, each drop answered as it lands, then all of them checked together with a count of how many are right.
- [Put the steps in order](tutorial-order), about 12 minutes. Six shuffled steps the learner moves up and down, from the mouse or the keyboard, checked against the right order with part marks, and restyled in one go.
- [Label the diagram](tutorial-label), about 20 minutes. A picture with empty spots the learner drags labels onto, checked spot by spot, with a text alternative so it works without seeing the picture.
- [Accordion and tabs from data](tutorial-accordion), about 20 minutes. Sections that open and close while the ones below move to make room, one open at a time, drawn from one list. Then the same list as tabs.
- [Mind maps and flowcharts from data](tutorial-diagram), about 20 minutes. A mind map whose branches fold and open on a click, and a flowchart learners follow one decision at a time, each drawn from a list by one box and one line.
- [A branching scenario](tutorial-branching), about 30 minutes. A situation with three choices, consequence pages that branch again, a path so far line and three endings picked by the score.
- [A memory card game](tutorial-memory), about 35 minutes. Twelve cards dealt face down from six pairs: turn two, keep a match, a miss turns back after a moment, with a moves count and a win page.
- [A "which one are you?" quiz](tutorial-personality), about 25 minutes. Three questions whose answers each add to several tallies, ending on the result page for the highest score.
- [Progress, badges and a page master](tutorial-progress), about 25 minutes. A panel on every page with a progress bar and badges that light up as tasks are done, drawn once on a page master.
- [A reflection journal that remembers](tutorial-journal), about 25 minutes. Two reflection prompts with text boxes and word counts that are still there when the learner comes back, and a page that puts it all together to download.
- [A poll with a live chart](tutorial-survey-chart), about 25 minutes. Learners vote on one question, then see a bar chart of how the whole class answered, counted and anonymous.
- [A what-if explorer (sliders and formulas)](tutorial-whatif), about 25 minutes. A weekly budget with sliders for hours, pay and rent, and the totals and a chart worked out again as the learner drags.
- [Personalise it for the course and the learner](tutorial-context), about 15 minutes. Greet the learner by first name, name their course, give each course its own due date, and show a line only to staff.
- [Interactive video with chapters and questions](tutorial-video-chapters), about 25 minutes. A video with chapter buttons that jump to each part, captions, and two questions that pause it at set times until they are answered.
- [Speaking practice with the microphone](tutorial-microphone), about 20 minutes. A one minute talk the learner records with one button, a countdown that ends the take, playback, and a slider to rate how it went.
- [Make it work on a phone](tutorial-responsive), about 25 minutes. A page of cards laid out by auto layout, and a narrow variant that stacks them for a phone. The player swaps between them by width.
- [Check and fix accessibility](tutorial-accessible), about 20 minutes. Make a small page with five common problems in it, then use the Accessibility checker to find and fix each one: contrast, names, keyboard, motion and Tab order.

## Advanced

- [An animated scene with speech bubbles](tutorial-scene), about 30 minutes. A layered scene where a character runs in and a speech bubble pops up for each speaker, with a Skip button, lines read to screen readers and a still version for less motion.
- [Word search and crossword from a word list](tutorial-wordgames), about 35 minutes. Puzzles made from a list you type, so the grid changes when the words do: how the Word search and Crossword starters work, and how to change them.
- [Content from a data file](tutorial-data-source), about 25 minutes. A page whose heading, text and cards all come from one JSON file, so next term's rewrite is a file swap, not an edit of every layer.
- [Drive an imported Rive or Lottie animation](tutorial-rive-drive), about 25 minutes. Import an animation made elsewhere, then put the learner in charge of it: a play and pause button, a knob that scrubs it, and your own controls for a Rive state machine.
- [Rig a character with bones](tutorial-bones), about 30 minutes. An arm drawn with the Pen, bound to three bones, that reaches for a dot you drag (IK) and waves when a button is pressed.
- [When you need a script](tutorial-script), about 25 minutes. A spelling question that accepts a near miss: a short sandboxed script does the one sum the nodes can't, and the graph and a binding do the rest. Also when not to write one.
