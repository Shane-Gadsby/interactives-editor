# The story as nodes
A chapter drawn as scenes, choices and endings joined by arrows, that you can edit, and the events the story sends as it plays.

**The Dialogue view:** **Dialogue** beside **Script** shows the chapter as nodes: each scene and run of lines, each choice with a way out per option, each if, and the endings, with arrows for where the story goes. Drag from a choice, or the right edge of a scene, onto another scene and the script gets the jump; **+ Scene** and **+ Choice** add them; **Delete** takes a node's lines out; click a node to edit its lines under the map. A scene nothing leads to is dashed. It's the same lines either way, so the two views never disagree.

**Reacting to the story:** **On story line** (everyone's, or one person's), **On story choice** and **On story scene** run in the Interactions graph as the story plays, for a portrait in a corner, a count, a sound, without a cue in the script.

**Good to know:**
- Nodes stay where you drag them (the chapter keeps their places), and new ones are laid out left to right in the order the story reaches them.
- Joining from a run of lines with no title gives it one (**New scene**), so there's somewhere to jump to. Rename it in the script.
- **Show in the script** takes you to the selected node's first line.
